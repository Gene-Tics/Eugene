Iris,


I know life gets busy. I know there are days when everything feels overwhelming and exhausting. I know there are moments when you're carrying more than people realize.


But I hope you never forget how much I appreciate you.


I appreciate your effort.
I appreciate your strength.
I appreciate your heart.


I love your smile.

And I love the parts of you that you don't always show people.


I love you during your happiest moments.

I love you during the quiet moments.

You never have to be perfect for me.


You never have to earn my love.


You are already enough.


Thank you for being you.


No matter where life takes us, I will continue choosing you, supporting you, and loving you.


Always and forever,

Ali ❤️
